# DECISIONS

Choices made where the brief was unclear or two items pulled against each other. Each one was settled by what best serves SEO, clicks and revenue.

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Moved the repo from Vite (browser-only rendering) to Next.js App Router**, in place. Old code is kept in `legacy/`. | The brief makes server-rendered pages non-negotiable; a Vite SPA ships an empty `<div>` to crawlers. Next gives static generation, per-page metadata, generated OG images and font self-hosting. Vercel detects it with no config. |
| 2 | **All public pages are statically generated**; nothing public waits on client JS. Filters, currency and gates enhance HTML that is already complete. | Crawlable and fast. The first page of creator results is always in the HTML and never gated. |
| 3 | **Five languages: English, Korean, Japanese, Chinese, Vietnamese.** English has no URL prefix; the others use `/ko`, `/ja`, `/zh`, `/vi`. | The brief says "5 languages" without naming them; these match the home markets with the most search demand. One line in `data/site.ts` changes the set. |
| 4 | **Header chrome is really translated; page body copy repeats English** in other locales. | The brief allows it. Routes, `hreflang` and dictionaries are real, so translation is a content job later, not a rebuild. |
| 5 | **Two-row header**: search, currency, language, log in and the one lime button on top; the nav underneath (it folds away on scroll). | Seven nav items plus a search-first bar don't fit one row at 1280px. Search stays visible at all times. |
| 6 | **Hero search filters the list right below it** instead of leaving the page; without JS it submits to `/creators`. | Seungchan's ask: visitors must reach creators immediately. One less page load before the first result. |
| 7 | **Categories sit as small icon links on the bottom line of the hero**, with quick chips under the search. | "Small icons, top right or under the search." Keeps the first screen about search. |
| 8 | **Local platforms are text, not logos.** Only Instagram, TikTok and YouTube get a logo; LINE, Naver, Douyin, Xiaohongshu etc. appear as plain names. Facebook is not in the data at all. | The brief limits logos to three and bans Facebook, but a market card for China or Japan is wrong without its local apps. |
| 9 | **Map labels appear on the active or hovered pin only**; all markets are also listed as flag chips above the map. | Seven Asian markets sit within a few pixels of each other at this scale; permanent labels collide. |
| 10 | **Client logos are typeset wordmarks marked `TODO: real logo file`.** | No logo files are in the repo, and recreating brand logos by hand would be wrong. LG H&H stays logo-only. |
| 11 | **Photos are neutral placeholders marked `TODO: real photo`.** Creator covers are flat colour tiles with the creator's own caption as cover text. | The brief bans AI-looking imagery. Typographic tiles look intentional and need no stock photos of real people. |
| 12 | **Every unset price renders as `$TBD`** straight from `data/pricing.ts`; nothing is invented. | Leadership should see exactly which numbers are still open. Filling a value in the config updates every page. |
| 13 | **`Offer` JSON-LD omits `price` while a price is TBD** (it is marked `PreOrder` / "Price to be announced"). | A fake price in structured data would be a false claim to Google. It switches to a real price automatically once the config has a number. |
| 14 | **Creator rates show the creator's currency first, the visitor's second** ("from ₫12M ≈ $472"). | One currency per rate card is the product rule; the conversion answers the buyer's real question. |
| 15 | **Currency detection is mocked** with saved choice → domain → browser time zone → USD. It never touches the URL. | No IP lookup in a mockup; and currency in the URL would split canonicals. |
| 16 | **Soft gate on search is a dismissible dialog**, shown once per visit after 20 results or 3 filter changes. | "Never block the first page." A wall would cost more sign-ups than it wins. |
| 17 | **Audience analytics gate counts per day in `localStorage`** (2 logged out, 10 for the brand account). | Mocks the real limit so the blurred state can be seen by opening a third profile. |
| 18 | **Country × category pages with fewer than 6 creators are `noindex, follow`**, left out of the sitemap and linked from their country hub. | Avoids thin duplicate pages while keeping them reachable. |
| 19 | **Creator profiles live at `/@handle`**, served by a rewrite to an internal `/profile/[handle]` route. | `@` is reserved in Next folder names. The public URL and canonical stay `/@handle`. |
| 20 | **Dummy accounts for every role** (brand, agency, creator, MCN, editor) on `/login`; one click signs in, in this browser only. | Requested during the build: every logged-in flow must be walkable with dummy data. Agency was added as its own role, since Agency Orders and the full Campaign Inbox belong to it. |
| 21 | **A "Review mode" dock** (bottom-left) holds the mockup-only switches: Preview as, theme, roadmap tags. | Keeps review controls out of the real UI. It is not part of the site to be built. |
| 22 | **Sales (GMV) has a disabled slot in three places**: a filter, a sort option and a line on each card. | The brief asks for a visible home for Commerce Data before it exists. |
| 23 | **Buttons have a hard navy edge that presses down**; no gradients, glows or glass anywhere. | The brief asks for flat lime / navy blocks that don't look AI-made. It also gives lime buttons a clear edge on the light page (contrast). |
| 24 | **Lime is never used as text on light backgrounds** — only as a fill with navy text, or as text on navy. | Lime on the page colour fails AA; both allowed pairings are above 13:1. |
| 25 | **Home page ships 84 of the sample creators**, the full set loads on `/creators`. | Keeps the home page HTML small for LCP while quick chips and filters still work. |
| 26 | **Review round, 19 Nov: the site uses the full page width** (content up to 1920px with side gutters), and the header is navy edge to edge on every page. | Requested in review. Row 5 still holds: two rows, search first. |
| 27 | **The hero is full-bleed navy with the moving collage** from the earlier mockup: five creator posts gliding between layouts, with a floating creator card. Search sits right under it; on phones search comes before the collage. | Requested in review ("follow the kappa site"). Search still has to be on the first screen. |
| 28 | **The home creator list is a spotlight plus tiles**: one featured creator framed like their own post, six tiles beside it, hover to feature. Every tile lists each platform the creator is on with follower counts. | Requested in review. Platforms per creator answer "where can I book them" without opening the profile. |
| 29 | **Collage and tiles keep flat colour with the creator's caption as cover text**, not the soft gradients of the earlier mockup, until real video is supplied (`video` / `poster` per item). | The brief bans AI-looking gradients; the caption text makes placeholders read as content. |
| 30 | **Logged-in workspaces are fully themed**: navy side menu with lime selection, navy screen headers, lime and navy KPI tiles. | Requested in review: the first version read as plain white. |
| 31 | **Dummy roles follow the "Logins, Roles & Pages" doc**: Brand, Creator, MCN, MegaEvolution team (platform admins, who run Agency Orders) and the planned video editor; team members use Owner / Admin / Manager / Member / Viewer. | Keeps the mockup in step with how accounts really work. The internal key for the team account is still `agency`. |
| 32 | **No Facebook or Google sign-in buttons** on `/login`; email + password and an email login code only. | The brief says no Facebook anywhere; the roles doc lists Facebook login on the live site. Flagged as an open question in HANDOFF.md. |
| 33 | **Second review round: the header is one row** (logo, nav, compact search with Ctrl K, one language/currency menu, log in, the lime button). Replaces row 5. | The two-row header read as clutter. Search stays one click or one shortcut away and sits large in the hero. |
| 34 | **The hero collage is replaced by an ordered wall**: three even columns of same-size creator tiles drifting slowly, each with the same four facts. Replaces row 27. | The free-form collage read as random. Same content, one clear pattern. |
| 35 | **Hero carries only headline, search, four quick chips, category icons and two text links.** Stats and the managers list moved out. | Less to read before the first action. The figures are in Proof; managers are on the map. |
| 36 | **Creator filters are category tabs plus one row of pills**; heading is one line with no sub-text. | The eight-field bar and two-line heading were heavier than the list they introduce. |
| 37 | **Tools are a pick-and-preview showcase**, and the Studio band has a draggable before/after with the edit timeline. | Plain cards and static blocks undersold both; these show the product instead of describing it. |
| 38 | **Section spacing cut by about a third** site-wide. | Review: too much blank space between sections. |
| 39 | **Creator tiles show the post the way its main platform does**: YouTube as a video card (thumbnail, duration, progress, title, channel line), TikTok as a vertical For You frame (side rail, @handle, caption, sound), Instagram as a post (header, image, action row, likes). | Third review: one generic tile didn't match how buyers recognise each platform. |
| 40 | **Every tile body has the same four rows** — who, stats, platforms, rate + save — so tiles in a row line up. | Third review: rate rows wrapped differently from card to card. |
| 41 | **The final "Ready when you are" band is replaced by a four-step brief form** with a live summary (category, markets, budget in the visitor's currency, local teams, matching sample creators). | Third review asked for a real contact form; it doubles as the agency inquiry on the home page. |
| 42 | **Footer link lists are one compact market grid** (each market: market page, all creators, its top three categories). | Same internal links for SEO in a third of the height. |
| 43 | **Creator heading, count, sort, category tabs and filters sit in one navy console**; the header search reads "Search creators" instead of showing Ctrl K. | Fourth review: the filter area looked plain, and the shortcut badge didn't say what the box does. Ctrl/⌘K still opens search. |
| 44 | **Header: five links plus "More"; search and language are icon buttons.** Replaces rows 5 and 33. | Fifth review: the one-row header was crowded. Connect your AI, case studies, pricing, blog, help and for-creators sit under More. |
| 45 | **Creators heading is a plain title with a live count; filters are one rounded toolbar** (search, categories, platform, a Filters panel, sort) with active filters as removable chips. Every creator card has a navy frame, lime when featured or hovered. Replaces row 43. | The navy console still read as heavy. Detailed filters live in a panel because most visitors only use category and platform. |
| 46 | **On the home page, clicking a creator tile features it in the spotlight**; the @handle and "View profile & rates" open the profile. The spotlight stops rotating once someone picks a creator. TikTok and Instagram spotlights are two columns (full-height post beside the details). | Review: clicking opened the profile instead of showing the creator, and the vertical post sat small in a grey box. |
| 47 | **Tools are one browser window**: a tab per tool, the sample screen, and the tool's steps, price and CTA beside it. Replaces row 37's list. | Review asked for something more creative than a list. |
| 48 | **Scrollable testimonials after Proof.** The two public client quotes, plus four placeholders labelled "Sample · replace". | Only two real quotes exist; the placeholders show the layout without passing as real clients. |
| 49 | **FAQ as numbered answer cards** beside a "Still have a question?" contact card. | Review: the plain accordion was flat. |
